HydroSmart is a building water-management concept: monitor total building consumption, shared tanks, water conditions, and system performance—with the goal of making water use easier to understand.

HYDROSMART / BUILDING WATER SYSTEM CONCEPT
The building-scale idea

One shared water system. A clearer picture.

HydroSmart focuses on the building’s overall water supply and shared infrastructure—not calculating consumption for individual flats.

≈

Building-wide consumption

Measure water passing through the building’s main supply to understand overall use.

▤

Underground and overhead tanks

Monitor underground storage and rooftop tanks to understand water availability.

◉

Water-condition monitoring

Use sensor readings as indicators that can prompt further water testing.

⌁

Leak awareness

Future leak sensors or unusual flow patterns could help flag possible leaks.

↗

Pressure monitoring

Additional pressure sensors could help track changes in building water distribution.

▥

Building-level reports

Bring total usage, tank status, and system alerts together in one overview.

Project goal

Make building-wide water use easier to understand, support better decisions about shared resources, and help people notice possible problems sooner.

Prototype and future direction

Starting small, thinking building-wide.

The current Arduino build is an educational prototype exploring sensors, water readings, an LCD, and pump control.

Current prototype

Hands-on water monitoring

Arduino Uno R3, ultrasonic level sensor, YF-S401 flow sensor, TDS and turbidity sensors, LCD, soil-moisture sensor, relays, and pumps.

Future concept

Building water management

Main-inlet building meter, monitoring of multiple tanks, pressure sensors, leak detectors, communications, and a shared dashboard.

Building-wide consumption, multiple-tank monitoring, pressure monitoring, and leak alerts are future goals; they are not all measured by the current prototype. Sensor readings alone do not certify that water is safe to drink.
